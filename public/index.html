Understand every important group in one minute a day.

WhatsApp handles the conversation. WALAO handles the understanding. It reads only the groups you switch on, then gives you one short brief a day — decisions, things waiting on you, and the dates that were agreed while you were busy.

Sign up See what it does

Notifications say something is new. They don't say whether it deserves you.

A busy group produces far more messages than outcomes. What you actually need is the answer to five questions:

  • What was decided today?
  • What is waiting on my reply?
  • Which meetings, payments and deadlines were mentioned?
  • What matters, but not right now?
  • Can I find an old decision without scrolling through hundreds of messages?

How it works

  1. Step one

    Connect a number

    Pair WhatsApp by scanning a QR code, the same way WhatsApp Web works. Use a spare number — see the note below.

  2. Step two

    Choose the groups

    Every group starts switched off. WALAO only ever receives new messages from the groups you enable, and you can turn one off again at any time.

  3. Step three

    Read one brief

    At the time and timezone you pick, each group is summarised and the results merge into a single Today Brief. Ask follow-up questions in your own words.

What a brief looks like

Good morning — 5 things worth your time today

Needs you (2)

  • Confirm the supplier meeting, Friday 3:00 PM Procurement
  • Reply on whether the final quote is approved Sales MY

Decided (2)

  • Packaging moves to option B Product
  • Launch date moves to 12 August Launch

Worth noting (1)

  • A customer says the login flow is too long Support

Every line opens to the messages it was drawn from. When a group has nothing worth reporting, WALAO says so instead of padding the brief.

Built to hold less of your data, not more

  • Sourced, not asserted

    Every claim carries the group, the time and the message it came from.

  • Off by default

    A group is not processed until you enable it, one at a time, on purpose.

  • You set the retention

    Raw messages are deleted after 1 to 30 days — whichever you choose.

  • Export or delete

    Take everything with you, or remove it all. Both leave an audit record.
