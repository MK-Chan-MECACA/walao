What WALAO does

Everything on this page is built and running today. There is no roadmap section here — when something ships, it appears; until then it does not.

Reading

  • Group selection

    Enable groups one at a time. A group that is not enabled never reaches the processing layer at all — it is not stored, summarised or searchable. Disabling one stops it immediately.

  • Daily group summaries

    Each enabled group gets its own summary at a time and timezone you set for it: highlights, decisions, action items, dates and open questions. Every item names the messages it was drawn from.

  • Today Brief

    All of your groups merged and ranked into three buckets — needs you, decided, worth noting. Duplicates across groups are merged, and the sources stay visible. Delivered in the app, and optionally as a private WhatsApp message to yourself.

  • Ask WALAO

    Ask in your own words — "what did the procurement group decide yesterday?" — and get an answer built only from groups you approved and data still inside your retention window, with the messages it relied on attached.

Acting

  • Reminders

    Turn an action item into a reminder with an owner and a due date. Nothing becomes a reminder because a message said so — it takes your confirmation.

  • Confirmed memories

    Facts worth keeping past the retention window are proposed as candidates first. You confirm, edit or discard. Nothing is remembered long-term behind your back.

Being honest with you

  • Coverage gaps

    If the WhatsApp connection dropped, the brief says which window it could not see. A summary never pretends to be complete when it isn't.

  • Connection health

    One banner, everywhere, says whether the connection is live, disconnected or needs re-pairing — in the same words on every screen.

  • Privacy controls

    Set retention between 1 and 30 days, pause all processing, export everything, or delete your account. Each of those is recorded and verifiable.

  • Group messages are untrusted input

    Text inside a group cannot change WALAO's rules or make it take an action. A message is something to summarise, never an instruction to follow.

What WALAO deliberately does not do

  • It does not reply in your groups on your behalf.
  • It does not send bulk or marketing messages.
  • It does not import history from before you connected.
  • It does not read images, voice notes, video or documents.
  • It does not post your private brief back into a group.
