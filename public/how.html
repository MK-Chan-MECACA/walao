The sequence
In words

How WALAO turns WhatsApp groups into one sourced brief.

The animation is not decoration for a product that does something else. Each beat is a thing WALAO actually does, in the order it does it.

Start free↗

01 · Input

Only the groups you switched on

Four group names, a message count each. Nothing else in WhatsApp reaches the system at all — not a direct message, not a group you left disabled.

02 · Analysis

The day's traffic, group by group

The particles converging are the day's messages. Each enabled group is summarised on its own schedule, in its own timezone.

03 · Sorting

Decisions and actions, separated from noise

Three clusters: what needs you, what was decided, what is merely worth noting. Most of the traffic ends up in none of them, which is the point.

04 · Sourcing

Every claim linked to its message

The selection box closing on one cluster is a question being answered. The answer carries the messages it was built from, so you can check it.

05 · Result

One brief, five things, one minute

Needs you, decided, worth noting — merged across every group, duplicates collapsed, sources still attached.
