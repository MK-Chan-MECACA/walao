Plans

WALAO is in private beta and there is nothing to pay yet. The limits below are the ones the product actually enforces; the prices are not set.

  • Free

    Enough to build the habit
    • 3 groups
    • 500 messages a day
    • 5 group summaries a day
    • Today Brief, Ask WALAO, reminders and memories
    • Retention you choose, 1–30 days

  • Pro

    For someone living in their groups
    • 20 groups
    • 5,000 messages a day
    • 50 group summaries a day
    • Everything on Free, with room to use it

    No published price. RM19–RM39 a month is a working research figure, not an offer.

  • Team

    Not available yet
    • Shared briefs across a team
    • Administration and audit
    • Longer retention policies

    Waiting on the enterprise security work it would need. There is no sign-up for it.

The trial

Every account gets 14 days on Pro's limits, starting the moment WhatsApp pairing completes. No card, nothing to cancel. When it lapses the account simply drops to Free's limits — your data, groups and briefs stay where they are.

Why there is no price yet

Charging for something that runs on an unofficial WhatsApp gateway would be selling you a promise the platform hasn't made. The beta is free while that risk is real and while we find out what the summaries are actually worth to people using them. When there is a price, it will be on this page before it is on a card statement.
